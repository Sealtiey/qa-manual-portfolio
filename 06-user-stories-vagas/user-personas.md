# User personas — Vagas.com

Duas personas (candidato e recrutadora) criadas para orientar novas funcionalidades hipotéticas da plataforma de vagas Vagas.com. Personas fictícias; as fotos de referência do documento original não foram incluídas. Conteúdo original em inglês.

## Lionel Andrade

**Occupation**: Junior Frontend Developer

**Location**: 25 years old, lives in São Paulo, Brazil

**Motivations**: Wants to find job openings that match his skills and experience. Seeks professional growth opportunities in innovative companies. Desires a simplified and transparent application process.

**Goals**: Secure a position at a company with good benefits and learning opportunities. Better prepare for interviews and technical challenges. Receive feedback on his job applications.

**Preferred Method of Communication**: WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Phone, Email.

**Social Networks**: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn.

**Expected feature**: “Personalized Technical Test” – A platform that allows candidates to take technical tests tailored to the desired job, providing a certification badge to boost their chances.

## Zoe Andrade

**Occupation**: HR Coordinator

**Demographics**: 38 years old, lives in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

**Motivations**: Wants to optimize the hiring process and reduce the time to hire. Needs to find qualified candidates quickly. Looks for tools that streamline resume screening and interviews.

**Goals**: Improve the candidate experience during the hiring process. Filter applicants more efficiently. Track recruitment metrics to refine hiring strategies.

**Preferred Method of Communication**: WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Phone, Email.

**Social Networks**: WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn.

**Expected features**: “Smart Talent Pool” – A system that stores and categorizes qualified candidates for future opportunities, using AI to suggest ideal profiles for new job openings.
