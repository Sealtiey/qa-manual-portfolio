# Características de qualidade (SQC) — elevador

Aplicação das características de qualidade de software (funcionalidade, confiabilidade, usabilidade, eficiência, manutenibilidade, portabilidade) a um objeto do mundo real. Conteúdo original em inglês.

**Item:** Elevator

**Short description of the item**: The elevator is a mechanical system designed to transport people or goods between different floors of a building. It consists of buttons for floor selection, doors that open and close automatically, and smooth movement between levels. The item is an elevator designed for buildings, not a construction elevator, which has easy portability. The elevator is fixed in place and serves as a means of transportation between floors.

### Functionality

- The elevator moves correctly between floors, and when the floor is selected on the button, it opens and closes the door.

### Reliability

- The elevator operates smoothly, without frequent failures or defects.

### Usability

- The buttons are intuitive, clearly labeled for each floor, and there are simple instructions for users.

### Efficiency

- The elevator moves efficiently between floors, the user does not have to wait long for it to arrive, and it consumes little energy.

### Maintainability

- The elevator has easy access for maintenance where parts can be easily replaced or repaired, and troubleshooting procedures are documented.

### Portability

- It does not have easy portability as it is fixed.
